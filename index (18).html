FAQS

Questions we hear often

Do I need to replace my floors?

Usually not. If the boards are structurally sound, refinishing restores the surface at a fraction of the cost and disruption.

How long does a refinishing project take?

It depends on the size of the space and the finish chosen. We give you a clear timeline in your estimate.

Can I change the color of my floor?

Yes. We can refresh the existing tone or move toward a lighter or deeper shade that suits your décor.

Do you work on kitchens?

Absolutely. Kitchen floors get a tougher, moisture-aware finish designed for spills and heavy use.
